# PacificVis Storytelling Benchmark, 2017–2026

This is the durable design benchmark distilled from the project handoff. Archive
coverage is imperfect: 2020 award labels were not confidently recovered, the
complete 2021 shortlist was unavailable, the 2025 winner is supported more
strongly by first-party author records than archive markup, and the 2026 winner
was not reliably identified. Preserve those qualifications in any originality
claim.

The defensible project claim is narrow: **no closely matching tsunami-warning or
hazard-arrival story was found in the recoverable PacificVis contest archive from
2017 through 2026.** Individual structural ingredients do have precedents.

| Year | Relevant benchmark | Project lesson |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2017 | *Bullies and Victims*; *Rescue Signatures in the Mediterranean* | Stage and annotate discoveries; a tool demo is not a story. |
| 2018 | *Two-stage Autoignition of n-dodecane* | A complex physical process can lead when made understandable. |
| 2019 | *Of Catastrophes and Rescues*; NOAA-based biodiversity entry | Scientific complexity should clarify a process, not advertise itself. |
| 2020 | *The Evolution of Putian Network* among recovered finalists | Coherent authored experience matters more than a library or chart type. |
| 2021 | *HoagsOLife* | A narrow subject can organize varied evidence. |
| 2022 | *Emergency Logistics*; *Polydodo* | Coordinate chapters and visual progression; keep modeling behind the message. |
| 2023 | *Twin Cloud*; catastrophe-reinsurance honorable mention | Let measurements determine visual form. |
| 2024 | *Shanghai's Lane*; *Starry Starry Night* | Use subject-specific art direction; guide first and explore second. |
| 2025 | *The Story of Wagyu*; likely *The Lighthouse* | One organizing visual system is stronger than unrelated clever graphics. |
| 2026 | ESG NLP honorable mention; air-quality and passenger-flow entries | Advanced analysis still serves a focused explanatory experience. |

## Cross-year criteria for this project

1. The travel-time grid generates the wavefront.
2. Each scene reveals one testable scientific relationship.
3. The interface resembles an oceanographic instrument or field record rather
   than a generic dashboard.
4. Map, clock, observations, and comparison panels behave as one system.
5. Optional exploration follows the communicated result.
6. One event and a few places beat a broad risk atlas.
7. Evidence and attribution are visible without taking over the narrative.
8. Technical sophistication is subordinate to the finding.

## Originality boundary

Do not republish the prior Pacific Climate Evidence Atlas as this entry. Reuse
its engineering pattern—Python preprocessing, React/TypeScript, D3 or MapLibre
where justified, static assets, accessibility, and static deployment—not its
published content or argument.

The detailed historical source links and entry notes remain in the original
handoff record supplied at project creation. Recheck official archives before
making a final competition-submission originality statement.
