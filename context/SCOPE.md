# Scope

## First complete version

One event; one modeled field; one distance-only baseline; a small DART and
tide-gauge chain; four to six community panels; one projection; one story clock;
one reproducible pipeline; one guided browser story; one compact evidence and
methods section.

## Feasibility gate

Production begins only after primary-source research and a data proof establish:

1. an authoritative modeled field with known format, extent, units, and terms;
2. at least two usable observations with documented time bases and station data;
3. a defensible arrival-pick method or authoritative picked arrivals;
4. event metadata that unambiguously links every source to the same event;
5. several analytically contrasting community candidates;
6. an honest definition for each timing shown as “warning” or “arrival” time.

## Deferred

Additional events, live data, operational claims, social vulnerability, routes,
backend services, large explorers, Bayesian or machine-learning methods, and 3D
or audio treatments. Add one only when a recorded decision names the evidence,
consumer, interface, acceptance criteria, and maintenance cost.
