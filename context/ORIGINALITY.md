## Correspondence received 2026-09-13

The project owner asked the PacificVis 2027 Visual Data Storytelling Contest
co-chairs whether a visual story and a paper may use the same compiled dataset
when they have different goals and contributions and their relationship is
fully disclosed.

Dr. Angelos Chatzimparmpas replied on behalf of the contest co-chairs. The
response said that using the same underlying dataset is acceptable in principle
when the submissions have clearly distinct goals and contributions and the
relationship is fully disclosed. It also said that a small amount of overlap
may be acceptable, while warning against substantial duplication of text,
figures, analyses, or other manuscript content because of self-plagiarism or
duplicate-publication concerns.

The co-chairs requested a later description of whether the interactive story
and paper are expected to overlap and, if so, the approximate type and extent
of that overlap. The original correspondence remains in the project owner's
mailbox and is not copied into this repository.

## Working overlap boundary

The present architecture expects overlap only in the shared scientific
foundation:

- public source datasets, source identities, and checksums;
- source-faithful normalized tables and common semantic contracts;
- provenance, units, time bases, datum/CRS records, rejection accounting, and
  source-factual data-quality or missingness facts; and
- a concise disclosure that both submissions use this common foundation.

The following remain submission-specific and may not be duplicated:

| Paper only | Story only |
| --- | --- |
| Multi-event analytical corpus | Selected single-event story bundle |
| Statistical and machine-learning models | Narrative thesis and scene order |
| Uncertainty method and model evaluation | Story pacing, annotations, and interaction design |
| Visual-analytics method or system | App-ready authored visual encodings |
| Paper figures, tables, results, and prose | Story figures, explanatory text, and submission abstract |

The exact amount of overlap cannot yet be quantified because the paper method,
paper figures, story thesis, and storyboard are not frozen. Shared source and
methods descriptions must be independently written for the needs of each
submission and explicitly cross-disclosed where required.
