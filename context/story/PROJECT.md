## Question

What visual comparison can honestly explain how one Pacific tsunami produced
different physical arrival-time experiences across a small observational chain?

The current distance-versus-arrival concept is a falsifiable candidate, not a
required conclusion. Story EDA may pivot to a stronger supported question, such
as uneven observability or the distinction among modeled, observed, and warning-
system clocks.

## Contribution boundary

This lane owns descriptive visual discovery, narrative selection, storyboard
state, story-specific visual grammar, compact reviewed story exports, and the
browser experience. It does not consume fitted paper models, paper feature
selection, paper figures, or the paper's visual-analytics method.

Shared source parsing, normalization, data quality, arrival definitions, and
reviewed releases remain owned by the root pipeline and shared context.

## Evidence states

- `preliminary_storyboard_evidence`: may use the accepted T-002C2 candidate
  build; supports discovery but no station promotion or public claim.
- `reviewed_story_evidence`: uses a T-002E-promoted shared release and has passed
  scientific and cartographic review.
- `promoted_story_primitive`: a reviewed result approved for storyboard or app
  export through a separate checksummed manifest.

## First deliverable

A small static diagnostic atlas containing a Pacific evidence map, observation-
coverage timelines, station time-series small multiples, and—if its spatial
contract passes—a distance-versus-contour diagnostic. Arrival residuals and the
community shortlist wait for the shared release gate.
